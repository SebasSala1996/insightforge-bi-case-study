# InsightForge: Senior BI Engineer Case Study Solution

Crafting business insight from raw operational data — a full-stack BI solution featuring dynamic dashboards, efficient ETL design, and strategic KPIs.

---

## 🧠 Project Overview

This project addresses a simulated business scenario that showcases the core competencies of a **Senior Business Intelligence Engineer**:  
Transforming messy source data into **clear, actionable insight** using advanced modeling and interactive Power BI visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Case Challenge

> The company needs better visibility into its operations, sales performance, and customer behavior to drive strategic decisions and operational efficiency.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI** – Data modeling & visualization
- **Power Query (M)** – Data transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated columns, measures, and KPIs
- **Excel/CSV** – Raw data source
- **Git** – Version control for documentation & assets

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 **Executive Overview**
- Key Metrics: Total Sales, Gross Margin, Avg. Order Value, Total Customers
- Trendlines and YoY growth comparisons
- Regional performance insights with map visualizations

### 🔹 **Sales Performance**
- Revenue and profitability by product, category, and region
- Monthly trend analysis and seasonal patterns
- Drill-through to specific sales reps or product SKUs

### 🔹 **Customer Insights**
- Customer segmentation by spend and frequency
- Top customers by revenue and lifetime value
- Churn signals and cohort performance

### 🔹 **Operational Efficiency**
- Order fulfillment and shipping delay KPIs
- Cost vs. revenue breakdown by channel
- Inventory turnover ratios
